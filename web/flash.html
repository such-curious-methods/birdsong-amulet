01 / Before you start

A minute of setup

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari and phones can't do this.
  • Use a USB-C cable that carries data, not just power.
  • Plug in one Amulet at a time.
  • If the Tone Atelier is connected in another tab, press Disconnect there first. Only one page can use the Amulet at a time.

Something not working? See problems and fixes.

02 / Update

Update, keeping your melodies

Installs the newest firmware and keeps the Call and Response you saved. When the installer asks about erasing the device, leave Erase unchecked.

This browser can't install firmware. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Installing needs a secure page. Open this page from its https:// address.

03 / Fresh install

Start over, erasing everything

This erases your saved melodies and colors. Use it only for a new board, or if an update didn't fix a problem.

This browser can't install firmware. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Installing needs a secure page. Open this page from its https:// address.

04 / Afterwards

Back to making sounds

Installing takes about a minute; keep the Amulet plugged in until it finishes. It restarts on its own. Then open the Tone Atelier and connect again.
