The panel harness

Four wires connect the board to the three panel controls. Every control shares the ground wire on pin 1.

  • Mark pin 1 on both halves of the connector. Dupont housings are not keyed. A reversed plug cannot short the battery, but the controls will misbehave.
  • The sliders have three terminals. Wire the common one (COM, usually the middle; check with a multimeter) to ground and one outer terminal to its signal. Cover the unused terminal with heat-shrink.
  • Never connect the unused Power terminal to 3V3 or to the battery's +.
  • Check which slider position closes the contact with a multimeter, then label the panel.

The battery

Check polarity before plugging in. Measure the battery's connector with a multimeter: red must match the + marking next to the FireBeetle's battery socket. A reversed battery can destroy the board's charger.

Use a protected 1S LiPo battery with a JST connector.

Room to grow

The two outer rows of holes on the carrier carry the same FireBeetle pins as the socket, so you can plug in your own sensors and parts. See getting started with ESP32.
